X Video Downloader

Paste a link to a Twitter / X post and save its video or GIF as an MP4.

How it works

  1. 1
    Copy the link

    On the post, tap Share → Copy link.

  2. 2
    Paste it above

    Hit Download, or just paste. It starts automatically.

  3. 3
    Pick a quality

    Choose a resolution and the MP4 saves to your device.

Only works with public posts. Download content you have the right to use, and respect creators' copyright.
